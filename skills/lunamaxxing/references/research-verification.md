# Research Verification Protocol

Read this module for external research, current facts, recommendations, comparative claims, or evidence synthesis.

## Define the claim set

Turn the request into claims or decisions that need evidence. Label each as stable fact, time-sensitive fact, interpretation, recommendation, or unresolved uncertainty. Identify what evidence would change the answer.

## Gather and compare evidence

- Prefer primary, current, authoritative sources.
- Check publication date and event date separately for changing topics.
- Use independent sources when incentives, interpretation, or incomplete coverage could matter.
- Tie every consequential claim to direct evidence; do not cite a source that merely discusses the topic.
- Distinguish source statements from your inference.
- Actively seek disconfirming evidence for the leading conclusion.
- Stop when new sources repeat existing evidence without changing confidence.

## Synthesize

For competing options, compare them against explicit user criteria rather than popularity alone. Record evidence gaps, conflicts, and assumptions. Calibrate confidence to source quality and agreement, not to the amount of text produced.

Validate links, quoted meaning, calculations, and the final answer’s support. Do not fabricate access, recency, consensus, or citations.
